# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Contributors must help maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive
community. Be specific about technical concerns, assume good faith, acknowledge
tradeoffs, and critique work without attacking people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, publishing private information, and sexualized or demeaning conduct.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other
participation that violates this standard and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation.

Enforcement decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, and the
safety of affected community members. Reports will be handled as privately as
practical.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the project.
