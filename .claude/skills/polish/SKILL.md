# Polish

Address evidence-backed details: optical alignment, hierarchy, typography,
wrapping, icon balance, hover/focus/press feedback, transition continuity,
loading/empty/error quality, button priority, copy clarity, and motion timing.

Make one hypothesis-driven pass at a time on the running product. Preserve
tokens, accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not add
effects merely to make the product feel “premium.” Re-capture evidence after
material changes.
