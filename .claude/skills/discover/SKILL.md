# Discover

Read existing product truth, then identify what is known, assumed, disputed, and
unknown. Investigate:

- actors, context, current behavior, workarounds, and pain;
- desired outcomes and success signals;
- constraints, dependencies, risks, and non-goals;
- opportunity areas and evidence quality.

Ask only questions that materially change scope or direction. Produce a concise
discovery brief with observations, opportunity map, assumptions, open questions,
research needs, and recommended next phase. Capture intent, not premature UI.
