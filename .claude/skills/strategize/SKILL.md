name: strategize

# Strategize

Separate facts, assumptions, constraints, and decisions. Produce:

- target users and priority jobs;
- desired behavioral and emotional outcomes;
- experience principles with concrete implications;
- primary journeys and moments that must feel exceptional;
- differentiation thesis and category conventions to preserve;
- information-architecture direction;
- accessibility, trust, privacy, and platform constraints;
- assumptions to test, risks, non-goals, and success measures.

Make tradeoffs explicit. If the strategy sounds universally agreeable, push
until it states what the product will prioritize and refuse.
