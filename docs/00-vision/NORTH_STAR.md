# North star

## Product

An opinionated product-building system that turns an idea into a distinctive,
researched, working vertical slice with evidence. Product research and design
create the visible advantage; the engineering harness makes it credible,
repeatable, and safe to extend.

## User

Founders, product engineers, designers, and enterprise teams who want the speed
of a coding assistant without accepting generic interfaces, lost context, or
unclear production authority.

## Pain

Most starters optimize either visual spectacle or engineering governance. Users
still have to invent the product workflow, art-direct vague prompts, understand
dozens of commands, and decide whether a polished demo is actually safe or
maintainable. The result is predictable UI, abandoned scaffolding, or expensive
rewrites after the first feature.

## Desired outcome

A newcomer can describe one outcome, decide whether current research matters,
see several genuinely different working answers, choose or combine a direction,
and build the first useful journey without learning the harness first. The
approved visual language becomes tokens, components, motion rules, evidence,
and durable decisions that survive later features and different coding clients.

## Immutable constraints

- Product-specific live output comes before framework education.
- One visible journey connects research, product, design, implementation,
  verification, and review; no capability is valuable merely because it exists
  in a catalog.
- Custom projects never inherit the starter's visual identity as their answer.
- References are ingredients; copying another product is not differentiation.
- A direction is not real until its content, interaction, responsive behavior,
  accessibility, reduced-motion behavior, and tradeoffs can be reviewed live.
- Human approval remains separate from generation, token compilation, code
  implementation, independent evaluation, deployment, and merge.
- External tools and skills may improve the experience but cannot become hidden
  prerequisites or silently expand authority.

## Success signal

- Median time from generated directory to the first live product-specific
  comparison is under ten active minutes, excluding downloads and client sign-in.
- At least four of five consented newcomers reach an approved direction and one
  verified useful feature without maintainer intervention.
- Every custom candidate passes the product-specific source and divergence
  contract; zero starter-demo or palette-only candidates qualify for approval.
- At least four of five independent design reviews score the selected direction
  4/5 or better for product specificity, hierarchy, interaction, and craft.
- After five feature changes, token, component, responsive, accessibility, and
  visual-regression checks show no unreviewed design-system drift.

These are launch gates, not current claims. Automated fixtures cannot substitute
for newcomer or independent design-review evidence.

## Anti-goals

- A gallery of starter themes.
- A prompt collection or marketplace of every available skill.
- A component dump whose default look becomes every downstream product.
- A design generator that optimizes screenshots while leaving dead interactions.
- An autonomous system that approves its own taste, permissions, deployment, or
  merge.
