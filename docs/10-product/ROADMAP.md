# Roadmap

Prefer outcome-based milestones over a feature dump.

## Now

- Enterprise golden path: guided domain intake, credible request/approval slice,
  generated engineering contracts, and local-versus-production boundaries.
- First-run usability: one command, progressive questions, one confirmation,
  one next action, and archetype-scoped evidence.

## Next

- Production adapter recipes for identity, transactional persistence,
  notifications/outbox, immutable audit storage, observability, and migrations.
- A second enterprise archetype only after the request/approval path is proven
  in real downstream projects.

## Later

- Organization policy packs, compliance-specific evidence templates, and
  managed internal catalogs.

## Explicitly not planned

- silently connecting production services or copying credentials;
- claiming local demo adapters are production authentication or authorization;
- bulk-installing every external skill, agent, MCP, or provider;
- shipping dozens of shallow templates instead of a few complete journeys.
