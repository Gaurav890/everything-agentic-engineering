# User-needs layers

## Functional

- See active, waiting, completed, and failed work.
- Understand dependencies and the current execution step.
- Review an approval with enough evidence to decide.
- Pause, resume, retry, or redirect a run.
- Open produced artifacts and source provenance.

## Emotional

- Feel in control rather than surprised by autonomy.
- Trust that consequential actions cannot silently pass.
- Avoid the anxiety of hidden work or ambiguous “thinking” states.
- Feel confident handing off monitoring to a colleague.

## Social and organizational

- Explain what the system did to reviewers and stakeholders.
- Preserve accountability without blaming an anthropomorphized agent.
- Demonstrate that approval, evidence, and policy boundaries were followed.

## Underlying motivation

The user does not want more agent activity. They want dependable leverage:
meaningful work progressing independently while decisions and accountability
remain human-readable.
